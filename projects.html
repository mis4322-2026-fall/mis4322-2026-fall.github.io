Four project deliverables

Deliverable percentages are shares of the project grade. The project remains 20% of the course grade.

1) Proposal

Due November 4, 2026 · 10% of the project grade (20 points).

Explain what you intend to build and how you will know it works. Organize the proposal clearly; the headings and format may vary.

  • Identify the project, the business problem, intended users, and value.
  • Describe the main workflow from user input to useful output.
  • Define a manageable scope with observable requirements, separating required features from future ideas. Use stable requirement IDs to connect your proposal, design, and video.
  • Include basic authentication: users can sign in, sign out, and access at least one protected application page. Registration and additional roles are optional unless your app needs them.
  • Describe the data, inputs, outputs, sources, and fictional examples you will use.
  • Define demonstrable acceptance criteria with sample situations and expected results, including authentication and restricted access.
  • Explain feasibility with Django and SQLite, dependencies, boundaries, risks, assumptions, and open questions. Include your own explanation of why the project is useful and achievable.

2) Design

Due November 11, 2026 · 15% of the project grade (30 points).

Translate your proposal into a plan for building and checking the application. Explain your choices and use diagrams or tables where they make the design clearer.

  • Identify the proposal you are designing from, scope changes, and unresolved decisions.
  • Show the system context, data model, and main user flow with diagrams. Describe fields, relationships, validation, and deletion rules where relevant.
  • Describe pages and actions: URLs, user inputs, validation, processing, and results. Connect them to proposal requirements.
  • Explain sign-in, sign-out, protected pages, and any roles or ownership restrictions. Distinguish application access from Django admin access.
  • Describe integrations, configuration, outputs, and business rules, or explain why they do not apply.
  • Plan small, demonstrable build increments. The first must be the base Django/SQLite project with working authentication and a protected application page, sufficient for the initial build deliverable. Add business features in later increments.
  • Map acceptance criteria to verification steps and the final video. Record risks, open decisions, and your explanation of how a request moves through the application.

3) Initial Build

Due November 18, 2026 · 5% of the project grade (10 points).

  • Submit the first build increment: a runnable base Django project using SQLite and Django’s built-in authentication. Business features can follow in later increments.
  • Demonstrate successful sign-in, invalid sign-in, sign-out, and a protected application page that anonymous users cannot access. Django admin login alone is not sufficient.
  • Include screenshots of the sign-in page, the protected page after signing in, and the access restriction after signing out. Keep passwords out of screenshots.
  • Explain what is working and what remains to meet your proposal requirements.

4) Video Demo

Due December 7, 2026 · 70% of the project grade (140 points).

Use the video to convince the instructor that your working application meets the requirements in your proposal. Highlight the design choices that made those requirements possible.

  1. Briefly introduce the problem, intended users, and proposed requirements.
  2. Demonstrate the running application. Walk through each proposal requirement and show evidence that it works.
  3. Show authentication and the main user workflows from start to finish.
  4. Highlight aspects of your design, such as the data model, screen layout, navigation, and access controls. Explain why you chose them.
  5. Identify any requirements that remain unmet and explain any differences from the submitted design.

Make the evidence easy to follow: connect each demonstrated feature to a proposal requirement and the relevant design decision.
